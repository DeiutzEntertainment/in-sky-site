IN-SKY.EU – OneBlock for Minecraft Java 26.3

mc.in-sky.eu: a vanilla-style OneBlock server where every island is its own private world.

Server address: mc.in-sky.eu
  • Java Edition
  • Version 26.3
  • Open beta
  • Co-op islands
  • Free to play

You start on a single block in the sky. Mine it, and it turns into another block. Keep mining, and the block moves through themed phases with new blocks, mobs and loot chests.

Your island is not a plot on a shared map. Each island is its own private world. Invite friends and build it together.

We are in open beta. Bugs can show up anywhere on the server. Players who joined early have already helped us find and fix bugs. If you find one, please report it at feedback.in-sky.eu.

Join the community on Discord: discord.in-sky.eu

Features

One block, many phases

The block changes every time you mine it. There are 21 themed phases, from Plains to Dappled Forest. Each phase has its own blocks, mobs and loot chests. After the last phase, it loops back to the start.

Every island is its own world

Each island is a separate private world. Other players' builds are not next to yours.

Play with friends

Invite friends to your island with /is team invite <player>. Teams can have up to 5 members.

Island ranks control what each person can do:

  • Owner – full control of the island.
  • Co-Owner – can kick, ban, trust players, change ranks, rename the island and set its spawn.
  • Member – can build, fight mobs and expand the border.
  • Trusted – can break, place and use blocks.
  • Guest – cannot build.

Owners and co-owners can ban players from their island and send visitors away.

Island protection

Each island has its own protection settings. During the beta, every island uses the same defaults:

  • Off: fire spread, fire burning blocks, creeper explosions and TNT explosions.
  • On: PVP, mob spawning, leaf decay and lighting fires.

PVP is on, so other players on your island can hurt you. If you don't want to fight, say so – please respect players who don't want to.

Keep-inventory is off, like normal survival. You drop your items when you die.

Grow your island border

Your island starts with a small border. While mining the block, you can sometimes find a rare "More Space!" item. Right-click it on your island to raise the border to the next tier. The whole server sees the message when someone finds one.

Island levels and Top Islands

Blocks you place on your island give points. Points raise your island level. Use /is level to see how far you are from the next level, and /is top to see the Top Islands leaderboard.

Your progress is saved

Your island, items and XP save automatically. When you join, you are sent straight to the server your island is on.

Vanilla-style

The game plays like normal Minecraft survival, just on one block. The server is free to play, and there is nothing to buy.

How to join

  1. Open Minecraft: Java Edition, version 26.3.
  2. Click Multiplayer, then Add Server.
  3. Type any name, for example "IN-SKY", and enter the server address mc.in-sky.eu.
  4. Click Done, select the server, and click Join Server.
  5. Type /is go to create your island.

Your game version must match the server version (26.3). The server is Java Edition only. Bedrock Edition is not supported.

Phases explained

Every block you mine adds 1 to your island's block count. When the count reaches the next phase's start, the block switches to that phase and your team gets a message. The scoreboard shows your phase, blocks mined and blocks left until the next phase. Use /is phases to see them all in a menu.

At 16,000 blocks the cycle loops back to Plains.

Commands

Type /is to see the full list in game. /island works too.

/is go
Create your island, or go back to it.
/is phases
See all phases and where you are.
/is team
See your team.
/is team invite <player>
Invite a friend. They accept with /is team confirm or decline with /is team deny. Invites expire fast, so accept right away.
/is top
See the Top Islands leaderboard.
/is level
See your island level and points to the next level.
/is count
See how many blocks your island has mined.
/is tier
See your island border tier.
/is info
Open the island info menu.
/is setspawn · /is setname
Set your island's spawn point or name.
/is trust · /is untrust <player>
Let a player build on your island, or remove that.
/is setrank <player> <member|co_owner>
Change a team member's rank.
/is kick · /is leave
Remove a team member, or leave a team.
/is ban · /is unban · /is banlist
Ban players from your island and manage the list.
/is expel <player>
Send a visitor off your island.
/is reset
Start your island over (owner only, asks to confirm).
/global
Turn global chat on or off.

Rules

There are almost no rules. You can play however you like, as long as you:

  1. Be nice. Always.
  2. Don't spam.
  3. Don't advertise. No marketing for other servers, products or channels.
  4. Leave people alone if they ask. If a player doesn't want to play with you, don't bother them.

FAQ

What is OneBlock?

OneBlock is a Minecraft survival challenge, first made as a map by IJAMinecraft. You start on one block floating in an empty world. When you mine it, it comes back as a different block. Over time you get new blocks, mobs and chests as you move through phases. You use what you get to build an island.

How do I join?

Use Minecraft: Java Edition 26.3. Go to Multiplayer, click Add Server, and enter mc.in-sky.eu. Join, then type /is go to create your island.

Is it free?

Yes. The server is fully free, and there is nothing to buy. You need your own copy of Minecraft: Java Edition.

Is the server in beta?

Yes. IN-SKY.EU is in open beta. Bugs can appear anywhere on the server. Early players have already helped us find and fix bugs, and your feedback helps too.

How do I report bugs?

Use the bug report form at feedback.in-sky.eu. You can also ask on Discord at discord.in-sky.eu. Tell us what happened, where, and what you expected.

How do islands work?

Every island is its own private world, not a plot on a shared map. You create one with /is go. Invite friends with /is team invite <player>. Each person gets a rank that controls what they can do. Your island grows when you find the rare "More Space!" item, and your island level goes up as you place blocks.

Can I play OneBlock with friends?

Yes. Invite them to your team, and you all share the same island, block and progress.

Can I play on Bedrock?

Not right now. The server is Java Edition only, version 26.3.

What are the rules?

Be nice, don't spam, don't advertise, and leave players alone if they ask. That's it.

What happens after the last phase?

After Dappled Forest, at 16,000 blocks, the block loops back to the Plains phase and the cycle starts again.

Will I lose my island?

Your island, items and XP save automatically. Because we are in beta, please report anything that looks wrong at feedback.in-sky.eu.
