A small update with one new feature: a scoreboard on the side of your screen, so you can see your progress without typing commands.

What the sidebar shows

  • Phase: the phase your block is in right now.
  • Blocks mined: how many times your island has mined the block.
  • Next phase in: how many blocks are left until the next phase.
  • Level: your island level.
  • Tier: your island border tier.

The sidebar updates every 5 seconds.
