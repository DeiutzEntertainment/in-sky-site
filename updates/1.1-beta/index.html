Update 1.1: Minecraft 26.3, new phases and safer islands

Version 1.1-BETA · 14 October 2026

Support for Minecraft Java 26.3, a new set of 21 phases that loops at 16,000 blocks, better island protection, a safer /is reset, and many fixes to invites and teams.

Our first big beta update. It moves the server to Minecraft Java 26.3, adds new phases, and fixes many problems early players found. Thank you to everyone who reported bugs.

Minecraft 26.3 and new phases

  • The server now runs Minecraft Java Edition 26.3.
  • There are 21 phases, including new ones such as Deep Dark, Lush Caves, Mangrove Swamp, Cherry Grove, Jagged Peaks, Sulfur Caves and Dappled Forest.
  • At 16,000 blocks, the block loops back to the first phase.
  • Fragile blocks now come in chests instead of from the block.

Safer islands

  • The OneBlock can't be pushed by pistons, destroyed by explosions or changed by mobs.
  • Portals are blocked on islands.
  • Item frames, paintings, armor stands, leads and vehicles are now protected like other blocks and items.
  • Damage from arrows and other projectiles now counts as coming from the player who shot them.

Teams and invites

  • Accepting an invite no longer clears your inventory before you have joined.
  • An invite can't be accepted twice, and it checks that the team isn't full.
  • To kick, ban or expel someone, you need a higher rank than them. The owner can never be kicked or banned.

Island reset

  • Only the island owner can use /is reset.
  • You must type /is reset confirm within 30 seconds to go ahead.
  • The warning now tells you that a reset clears your inventory, ender chest and XP.

Other fixes

  • Your health and hunger now stay the same when you move between our servers, like your inventory.
  • The "More Space!" border item works again.
  • New islands are named after their owner, for example "Steve's island!".

Found a bug? Report it at feedback.in-sky.eu or tell us on Discord.
