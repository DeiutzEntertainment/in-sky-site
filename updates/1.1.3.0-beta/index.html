Update 1.1.3.0: island ranks, trusted players and permissions

Version 1.1.3.0-BETA · 24 October 2026

Give friends a rank, trust players to build without joining your team, and choose which rank can do what on your island. Plus more ores and diamonds in several phases.

This update is about who can do what on your island. Owners and co-owners can now give players ranks, trust players to build, and change what each rank is allowed to do. We also added more ores and better loot to several phases.

Island ranks

Every player on your island now has a rank: Owner, Co-Owner, Member, Trusted or Guest. A player with no rank is a Guest.

  • /is setrank <player> <member|co_owner> changes a team member's rank. Stand on your island when you use it.
  • You can't change your own rank, the owner's rank, or the rank of someone above you.
  • Both players get a message when a rank changes.

Trusted players

Want a friend to help build without joining your team? Trust them.

  • /is trust <player> lets a player break, place and use blocks on your island.
  • /is untrust <player> removes that again.
  • Trusted players don't count toward the team limit of 5 members.
  • Banned players can't be trusted. Unban them first.

Choose what each rank can do

Type /is permissions to open a menu with one book per permission. Left-click a book to raise the rank it needs. Right-click to lower it.

  • /is permissions set <permission> <rank> sets it by command.
  • /is permissions reset <permission> puts it back to the default.
  • /is permissions list shows every permission and its rank.

By default, co-owners and the owner can change permissions. You can only change a permission if it doesn't need a rank higher than your own.

More ores and better loot

  • Underground: diamond ore added, and more iron ore.
  • Dungeon: all ore types added, including deepslate ores. New chests with diamonds, or with obsidian, a lava bucket and gold ingots.
  • The Nether: iron and diamond ore added, plus the obsidian, lava bucket and gold chest.
  • Deep Dark: deepslate iron and deepslate diamond ore added.
  • Ocean and Jungle: a new rare chest with oak logs, diamonds and gold ingots.

Small fixes

  • When you invite someone, the message now shows their name.
  • When you kick someone, the message now shows who you kicked.
