Updates

What changed on IN-SKY.EU, newest first. We are in open beta, so updates come often.

  • Update 1.1.3.0: island ranks, trusted players and permissions

    Version 1.1.3.0-BETA · 24 October 2026

    Give friends a rank, trust players to build without joining your team, and choose which rank can do what on your island. Plus more ores and diamonds in several phases.

  • Update 1.1.1: sidebar scoreboard

    Version 1.1.1-BETA · 16 October 2026

    A new sidebar shows your phase, blocks mined, blocks until the next phase, island level and border tier.

  • Update 1.1: Minecraft 26.3, new phases and safer islands

    Version 1.1-BETA · 14 October 2026

    Support for Minecraft Java 26.3, a new set of 21 phases that loops at 16,000 blocks, better island protection, a safer /is reset, and many fixes to invites and teams.
